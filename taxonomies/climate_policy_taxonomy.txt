TAXONOMY OF CLIMATE POLICY NARRATIVES

==================================================
CODING STRUCTURE
==================================================

Each response receives:
  A1  Restrict / Regulate          (0 or 1)
  A2  Price / Incentivize          (0 or 1)
  A3  Invest / Build               (0 or 1)
  A4  Inform / Educate             (0 or 1)
  A5  Adapt / Protect              (0 or 1)
  A6  International / Coordinate   (0 or 1)
  A7  Skeptical / No action        (0 or 1)

==================================================
SECTION 1 — POLICY LOGIC CODES
==================================================

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A1  RESTRICT / REGULATE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mechanism: legal compulsion or prohibition imposed on actors.

Signal words: ban, restrict, make illegal, enforce, fine, penalise,
hold accountable, standards, limits, mandatory, quota, prohibit,
oblige, sanction, strictly control, require, obligation.

Examples:
· "Factories must treat waste before discharge"
· "Heavy fines for illegal logging"
· "Mandatory emission quotas for polluting industries"
· "Limit the number of flights per person per year"
· "Oblige companies to use only renewable energy"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A2  PRICE / INCENTIVIZE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mechanism: price signal; government steers private behaviour via cost.
Includes taxes on harmful activities, subsidies or rebates for clean
alternatives, and removal of fossil fuel support.

Signal words: carbon tax, tax, subsidise, rebate, incentive, make
cheaper, make more expensive, remove subsidies, eco-malus, duty,
tariff, financial reward, free public transport (as incentive),
tax credits, penalise financially.

Examples:
· "Implement a carbon tax"
· "Subsidies and rebates for electric vehicles"
· "Make train travel cheaper than flying"
· "Remove fossil fuel subsidies"
· "Tax polluting companies much more heavily"
· "Financial incentives for households to install solar panels"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A3  INVEST / BUILD
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mechanism: government as direct provider or funder of a public good,
infrastructure, service, or natural capital.

Signal words: invest in, build, fund, plant trees, deploy, develop,
create, expand, construct, nationalise, public spending, R&D,
research funding, provide, establish, set up.

Examples:
· "Invest in renewable energy infrastructure"
· "Build more public transport"
· "Plant trees and restore forests"
· "Fund R&D for clean technology"
· "Develop green energy projects"
· "Establish more waste recycling facilities"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A4  INFORM / EDUCATE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mechanism: information and communication as the primary lever.
Includes awareness campaigns, formal education, and public knowledge
production.

Signal words: educate, raise awareness, awareness campaign, teach in
schools, public communication, sensitise, inform citizens, research
for public use, declare a crisis (as communication act).

Examples:
· "Run awareness campaigns in schools and public spaces"
· "Teach climate change in school curricula from primary level"
· "Declare climate change a public health crisis"
· "Disseminate information widely through multiple channels"
· "Raise public awareness about climate change"
· "Sensibiliser le public"
· "Nâng cao y thuc cong dong"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A5  ADAPT / PROTECT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mechanism: reducing vulnerability to climate impacts already occurring
or projected. Coping rather than preventing.

Signal words: sea walls, flood protection, drought resilience, heat
resilience, relocate communities, climate-proof infrastructure, early
warning systems, prepare for extreme weather, coastal protection.

Examples:
· "Build sea walls and flood defences for coastal cities"
· "Develop drought-resistant crops"
· "Climate-proof new buildings against rising temperatures and flooding"
· "Invest in early warning systems for extreme weather events"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A6  INTERNATIONAL / COORDINATE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Mechanism: supranational action or coordination as a necessary
component of the government's response. The respondent proposes that
the relevant unit of action is international — through treaties,
multilateral institutions, or pressure on other countries.

Signal words: international agreement, global cooperation, UN, COP,
all countries must act, lead by example, pressure other countries,
multilateral, bilateral, diplomatic, other countries too.

Examples:
· "All countries need to act together through a UN agreement"
· "We should lead by example so other nations follow"
· "Support and strengthen COP commitments"
· "Pressure China and the US to reduce emissions"
· "What's the point if other countries don't act too"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────
A7  SKEPTICAL / NO ACTION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────
Respondent does not think government should act on climate change —
because it is natural, a hoax, already too late, or because markets
or technology will resolve it without government intervention.

Signal words: natural cycle, hoax, nothing we can do, technology will
solve it, damage is done, government shouldn't interfere, bigger
priorities, markets will fix it.

Examples:
· "Climate change is a natural cycle; government shouldn't intervene"
· "The damage is already done"
· "Technology from private firms will solve this; no need for government"
· "People aren't ready to change their comfort"
